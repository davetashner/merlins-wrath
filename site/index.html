TheVesper Bell

For three weeks, the people of Briar Glen have been walking out of their beds at night and not coming back. Something beneath the mine is singing.

In development

The Vesper Bell is a fantasy action RPG for the desktop browser. Play as a knight, archer, sorcerer or thief, and find that each one opens different doors: a wall to smash, a mechanism to shoot, a spell that freezes the river, a rooftop no one is watching.

Beneath the town lies a labyrinth, a minotaur the town calls a monster, and a bell that hasn't rung in sixty-one years.
