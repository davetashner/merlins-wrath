## Decision

One paragraph: what we decided, stated so a reader who stops here knows what to do.

## Context

Why a decision was needed now: the forces, constraints (link `CONSTITUTION.md` and the backlog contract
sections that apply) and what happens if we do nothing.

## Options considered

| Option | Summary | Result |

## Evidence

What we measured or checked, how (commands, scripts, versions, machine) and the results. Prefer tables.
Link committed scripts and data so the evidence can be reproduced. Say what was *not* tested.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier or harder, and which beads, contract rows or docs change because of this.
- Costs we accept.

## Revisit triggers

Concrete, observable conditions under which this decision must be reopened (not "if it stops working").
